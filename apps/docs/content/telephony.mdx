# Take reception calls with your team

Open **Surgery Disconnect** from the Riverside Practice desktop. Connect to the same team world as your colleagues, then enter your receptionist name. Each open call desk appears as a separate team member.

1. Choose a caller from the queue and select **Answer call**.
2. Enable caller voices to hear the caller through your browser's speech engine. Use **Replay voice** to listen again. The transcript remains available when audio is unavailable.
3. Select **Open in SystemTwo** to open that caller's patient record in another tab. Keep the call desk open to remain connected.
4. Hold the call, transfer it to an available colleague, or record a note before ending it.
5. Use **End call & next** to finish the call and answer the next waiting caller in one action.

The team list shows who is available, away or handling a call. Another receptionist cannot answer a call that you own. A disconnected receptionist's call returns to the queue. Completed calls retain the receptionist's name and closing note.

## Verify a shared call desk

Run `node scripts/verify-telephony.mjs` against a running application. The check opens two receptionists in one world and another in a separate world. It tests simultaneous answers, ownership, transfers, hold and resume, end-and-next, disconnect recovery, and patient record lookup. `pnpm smoke` includes this check.

Run `node scripts/verify-telephony.mjs --restart` to also restart the local Compose application and verify that completed calls and notes survive in PostgreSQL.

## Connect a client

The WebSocket endpoint is `/api/telephony/live` on the application's existing origin and port. The first message authenticates with a GP-scoped team key and a receptionist name. Do not place the API key in the URL.

The executable message schemas are `TelephonyClientMessage` and `TelephonyServerMessage` in the <a href="/api/openapi.json">API catalogue</a>. Each command has a request ID. Call changes use the latest call ID and version from a snapshot. Acknowledgements and errors identify the request. Snapshots describe the committed queue and connected team members.

Call audio uses the browser's installed speech voices. Voice selection and audio availability vary by browser and operating system. Calls and transcripts are generated within NHS-SIM and do not connect to a phone network.
