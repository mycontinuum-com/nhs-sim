## Two workplaces, one patient journey

Open <a href="/control/">the neighbourhood map</a>. Choose Riverside Practice for SystemTwo, or Northbank General for Millbank EPR. The map gets out of the way inside each workplace.

Community, pharmacy, diagnostics and referrals support these journeys through records and APIs. They have no separate portals.

One API key opens one isolated world. The portals and APIs read the same records. Actions can reserve capacity, schedule work, change ownership, or make a record visible to another service. Advancing the clock lets you observe delayed results.

## Inside the record

In SystemTwo, use **Find patient** to search by name or identifier. The **Journal** holds dated entries; **Problems**, **Medication** and **Coded history** let you inspect the larger EHR collections. Select a record to open its details.

Millbank opens on a **Worklist**. Choose a patient, then use **Results**, **Medication** or **Documents** to inspect their record. The **Discharge chart** groups GP handover, pharmacy supply and community follow-up.

Both workplaces offer **Request test**, **Prescription** and **Home visit** actions. Use **Neighbourhood map** in the title bar to change workplaces.

Use the portals to inspect what your code changed. A successful request is the beginning of the workflow. Show the completed result in your demo.
